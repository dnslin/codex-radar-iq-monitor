# Provider icons

These eight brand symbols are copied unchanged from
[`@lobehub/icons-static-svg` 1.95.0](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@lobehub/icons-static-svg/v/1.95.0),
part of [Lobe Icons](https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons).
They are stored locally so the extension does not request images from a CDN.
The upstream MIT copyright and permission notice is included in [LICENSE](LICENSE).
Brand names and marks belong to their respective owners; inclusion here identifies
the model providers and does not imply an official partnership or endorsement.

Every SVG retains its original `viewBox` and aspect ratio. OpenAI, Grok and Z.ai
are transparent monochrome symbols, displayed in black or white according to the
theme. The other symbols retain their brand colors. Kimi's white symbol and blue
dot sit on a dark backing in both themes so the white strokes remain visible.
Z.ai's current Z symbol is used for the GLM group, rather than the older dotted
Zhipu mark.

`all.svg` and `other.svg` are simple project-created navigation symbols, not brand
logos. The extension's own radar app icon is separate from these provider assets.
